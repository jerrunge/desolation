Desolation Wilderness — Late June

Jeremy & Jeremiah  ·  Eagle Falls → Dicks / Fontanillis → Dicks Pass → Lake Aloha  ·  2–3 nights

A granite-and-alpine-lakes loop out of Emerald Bay into the most dramatic core of Desolation. You sleep night one in the Dicks / Fontanillis cirque (your secured permit zone), cross Dicks Pass for the big views, and drop to iconic Lake Aloha. Three ways to run it depending on time and whether you want to shuttle.

Snow year is in your favor2026 ran ~51% of normal and melting fast. The high country should be largely clear by late June — but Dicks Pass (9,380 ft) is the one spot snow lingers. Pack microspikes and confirm pass conditions with the LTBMU office the week before.
Bugs are the real bossLate June in a freshly-melted basin = peak mosquito. Treat clothing with permethrin ahead of time and carry head nets. Non-negotiable.
No backcountry signal — and don't count on UberMarginal cell at the trailhead (line-of-sight to South Shore), zero service in the basins. Carry a satellite messenger (Garmin inReach or iPhone 14+ satellite) and leave your itinerary + exit time with David. Uber/Lyft run in town but are unreliable at the trailhead — pre-arrange transport.
Fish the right lakesAloha is the scenery showstopper but a shallow dammed lake that fishes poorly. Put rod time into Dicks, Fontanillis, the Velmas, Susie, and Gilmore instead.

2-Day / 1-Night

Fast, light alpine overnight in the Dicks/Fontanillis cirque. Best if time is tight.
Shuttle / Point-to-Point

Same highlights as the 3-day but a gentler one-way finish. Needs a second car staged at Glen Alpine (~10 min away) or a pre-booked taxi.

Every option starts the same: night one in the Dicks / Fontanillis cirque to match your Zone 23 permit. The permit only governs the first night — you roam freely after.

Option B — 3-Day / 2-Night (recommended)
Day 1~6 mi · +1,900 ft
Eagle Falls TH → Eagle Lake → Velma Lakes → Dicks / Fontanillis cirque (~8,400 ft). Steep granite climb onto the plateau. Afternoon: fish Dicks & Fontanillis.
Camp: Dicks or Fontanillis Lake (Zone 23)
Day 2~7–8 mi
Cirque → Dicks Pass (~9,380 ft) — the big-views day, north to the Velmas, south to Half Moon, Susie & Aloha → descend to Susie → Heather → Lake Aloha. Fish Susie; side-trip Gilmore.
Camp: Susie or Heather Lake (~7,800–7,900 ft)
Day 3~10–11 mi
Morning at Aloha / Gilmore, then back over Dicks Pass → past the cirque & Velmas → Eagle Lake → trailhead. Big exit day.
Exit: Eagle Falls TH
Option A — 2-Day / 1-Night
Day 1~6 mi · +1,900 ft

Optional dawn climb toward Dicks Pass for the panorama, then retrace past the Velmas → Eagle Lake → trailhead.

Option C — Shuttle / Point-to-Point (2-Night)
Day 1~6 mi · +1,900 ft
Eagle Falls TH → Velma Lakes → Dicks / Fontanillis cirque. Fish Dicks & Fontanillis.
Camp: Dicks or Fontanillis Lake (Zone 23)
Day 2~7–8 mi
Cirque → Dicks Pass → Susie → Heather → Lake Aloha. Camp near Gilmore or Heather to set up the downhill exit.

Susie / Heather → Gilmore Lake → Glen Alpine TH. No Dicks Pass re-climb — a gentle one-way finish.

Shuttle setupStage a second car at Glen Alpine (~10 min by road from Eagle Falls) before you start, or pre-book a South Lake Tahoe taxi with a confirmed pickup time — don't rely on summoning an Uber from the trailhead.

Drive & access
Route: Pacifica → I-280/I-580/I-205 → US-50 east through Placerville to South Lake Tahoe → CA-89 north ~10 mi along the lake to the Eagle Falls / Emerald Bay trailhead. ~3.5–4 hr (~190 mi).
Parking: The Eagle Falls lot is small and fills early — arrive at first light or use the overflow along CA-89. Day-use parking fee applies (bring cash/card).
Glen Alpine (shuttle exit) is ~10 min by road from Eagle Falls, off Fallen Leaf Lake Rd.
Permit & regulations
Zone 23 — Dicks (secured). Desolation permits are tied to your first night's zone; after that you camp anywhere. Print it or keep it in the Recreation.gov app (offline).
Bear canister required — not a bag. Hard rule in Desolation.
Campfires prohibited — stove only.
Max group size 12. Dogs on leash OK (but you've no dog along).
Walk-in / changes: LTBMU Supervisor's Office, South Lake Tahoe, handles same-day permits & questions.
Connectivity & rides
Cell: a bar or two at the trailhead (line-of-sight across the lake, carrier-dependent), none in the basins. Treat the backcountry as fully off-grid.
Satellite: carry a Garmin inReach or use iPhone 14+ Emergency SOS / Messages via satellite. Leave the itinerary + expected exit time with David.
Uber/Lyft: run in South Lake Tahoe but unreliable at the trailhead. Don't make it your exit plan — pre-arrange the shuttle or run two cars.
Fishing
License: CA sport fishing license required for both of you (16+). Buy online via CDFW; short-term licenses available — keep a screenshot offline.
Best water: Dicks (rainbow), Fontanillis, the Velmas, Susie, and especially Gilmore. Skip Aloha — shallow, dammed, poor fishing.
Gear: ultralight spinning rod with ½-oz spinners (Panther Martin, Mepps) or a Tenkara fly setup. Work the rocky drop-offs into deep water.
Safety & emergency
Hospital: Barton Memorial Hospital, South Lake Tahoe.
Crux hazard: Dicks Pass snow/ice on the north side early season — microspikes + an early start over the pass.
Altitude: mild (max 9,380 ft), but Jeremiah's coming from Indiana — stage a night near the lake beforehand and ease into Day 1.
